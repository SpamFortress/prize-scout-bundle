# Verification checks

These checks run on a fetched sponsor page. They do not call Brave, and they do not look up a sponsor in an outside registry.

A later option is an external sponsor registry or a BBB lookup. This build does not call one.

1. **Sponsor identity.** Verified needs a sponsor the rules tie to this entry. The name may match the host, a known promotion vendor (Gleam, ViralSweep, KingSumo, SweepWidget, RafflePress, Woobox, and the other hosts in `PROMOTION_VENDOR_HOSTS`), or the owner’s source list. A parent company counts when the rules say so: “d/b/a”, “a division of”, “operator of this website”, or the rules tell you to enter at this host. A sponsor named with a postal address on the same site as the entry also counts. Otherwise the row stays in Needs checking with “Sponsor not confirmed”, including when no sponsor is named, the named sponsor is unrelated and no tie is stated, or the prize is $5,000 or more on an unrecognized domain with no tie.
2. **Rules versus entry page.** When the rules page and the entry page both state an end date, a prize, or a promotion year, and those facts differ, the row is not Verified. The note is “Rules and entry page disagree (possible reused rules)”. A month and day with no year is not compared, and no year is filled in.
3. **Where Enter goes.** Verified is blocked when the form asks for a bank account, card number, or SSN before a win. Post-win W-9 or tax language is not a block. Verified is also blocked when Enter goes through a URL shortener or an affiliate redirect, or lands on the brand homepage, a bare PDF, a winners page, or a sweepstakes directory. The note names that issue. `utm_` and other click ids are removed from stored links.
4. **Freshness.** A fetched row stores `lastChecked` and the list shows “Last checked &lt;date&gt;”, including Checked by you. A stored sponsor page is fetched again when that date is more than 7 days old, or the end date is within 3 days. The fetch is the sponsor page, not a directory and not a Brave search. If the page errors or the form is gone, Verified drops to Needs checking with “Entry form not found on last check (&lt;date&gt;)”.
5. **Sourcing.** The two platform searches are unchanged in count. The second one also asks for official-rules pages and paths such as `/official-rules`, `/sweepstakes`, newsroom, and press. Hits on sweepstakesradar, sweepsheet, sweepfeed, sweeptakers, contestgirl, sweepsadvantage, online-sweepstakes, ultracontest, and sweepstakesfanatics are dropped and are not saved as listings.
